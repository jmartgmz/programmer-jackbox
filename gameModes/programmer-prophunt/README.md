The programmer prophunt is a gamemode where, upon startup, the current lobby members are split into two equally sized teams.

The host may set the complexity, time limit, and number of rounds.

Each round, one team will be given a complete block of code. To this code, each member must add exactly one new line and confirm it. These lines are visible to all other members of their team.

Once every member of the hiding team has written their line, the updated block will be made visible to the other team.
Each member of this team, the "finders", must select a line they think stands out or is unusual in some way.

Once each finder has confirmed their selection, the finder team will get a point for each correctly identified hider they call out, while the hiders will get a point for each member that was not called out.

If a hider or finder fails to act within the time limit of their turn, their team will be deducted one point.
