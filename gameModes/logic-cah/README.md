Each round, a decider will be chosen and will change each round until each person goes in a circle.
The host, to initialize the game, can select the number of rounds (number of times each person in the lobby is the decider), the time limit, and the number of prompts.

Each turn, a collection of prompts will be made visible to all players, and every non-decider must enter an answer they feel makes logical sense in every single one of them simultaneously.

The decider chooses the response they most agree with, as these are displayed anonymously. Once the time limit runs out, these responses are displayed to them anonymously and the decider chooses. The names are then revealed after the decider confirms their choise (not switching for 10 seconds) and the selected person gets a point.
